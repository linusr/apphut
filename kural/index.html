Kural Podcasts

குரல் — "voice"

Kural is a native podcast player for iPhone and Apple Watch. Follow shows from Apple's podcast directory or any RSS feed; your library, progress and Up Next sync across your devices through iCloud. If you run a self-hosted PinePods server, Kural syncs with it too.

  • Up Next queue that clears episodes once you've heard them
  • Background downloads, offline playback and Skip Silence
  • Automation rules that download, queue and clean up on a schedule
  • OPML import and export
  • Optional Apple Music: search, playlists, recommendations and favorites
  • CarPlay with Up Next, your shows, the latest episodes and music
  • Home and Lock Screen widgets, and an Apple Watch remote with a complication
  • No accounts with us, no analytics, no tracking

Kural is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the PinePods project.
