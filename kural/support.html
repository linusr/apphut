Support

For help, bug reports or feature requests, open an issue on GitHub. Include your iOS version, the app version shown at the bottom of Settings, and, if you use one, your PinePods server version.

Getting started

  1. Open Kural and tap Find Podcasts, or use the Search tab: search Apple's podcast directory or paste a feed URL, then tap +.
  2. To bring your subscriptions from another app, export OPML there and choose Settings → Import OPML.
  3. To sync with a PinePods server, choose Settings → Sign In to PinePods. Shows already on your iPhone are added to the server.

Common questions

My library doesn't appear on my other devices.
Sync uses iCloud. Check that you're signed in to the same Apple Account on each device and that iCloud is on for Kural. Settings → Sync shows the iCloud status.

What syncs with PinePods?
Shows, playback positions, played and saved episodes, and Up Next, in both directions. YouTube channels and server downloads are available only while signed in.

Why was an episode removed from Up Next?
Episodes leave the queue once they're played, on any device or in PinePods.
