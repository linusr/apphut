Naal

Calendar & Tasks, arranged for your day

Coming soon to TestFlight. A public beta link will appear here.

Naal brings your calendars, Apple Reminders and contact birthdays into one view of your day. Instead of a grid of hours, it arranges what matters into tiles: the next meeting with a countdown, today's agenda, free time, birthdays, a map of where you're going, and trips gathered together. On devices with Apple Intelligence the arrangement is decided on the device itself.

  • An arranged view of your days, and a timeline that keeps the selected day in the middle
  • Tasks from Apple Reminders by day, with swipe to complete or move, and filters by list
  • Repeat after completion: write "Repeat 2 weeks after completion" in a reminder's notes and the next one is due two weeks after you finish it
  • Live Activity for the next event, with Join and Directions, and back-to-back meetings shown as one run
  • Home Screen widgets
  • Works with the calendars and reminders already on your devices; no account with us, no analytics, no tracking
