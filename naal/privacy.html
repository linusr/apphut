Privacy Policy

Effective October 12, 2026

Naal shows your calendars, reminders and contact birthdays as an arranged view of your days. The developer of Naal does not operate a service for the app, does not collect any data from it, and has no access to your calendars, reminders, contacts or usage.

Data the app does not collect

Naal contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking code, and sends nothing to the developer.

Data the app reads

  • Calendars, to arrange and show your days, and for widgets, the Live Activity and event notifications. Required.
  • Reminders, for the Tasks view and to complete, reschedule and repeat reminders. Optional.
  • Contacts, only for names and birthdays, to show the birthdays coming up. Optional.

This data is read on your device and is not copied anywhere else by Naal.

Data the app writes

  • When you set a reminder to repeat after completion, Naal adds a line such as "Repeat 2 weeks after completion" to its notes and creates the next reminder when you complete one. These sync through your iCloud account like any reminder.
  • Naal keeps a small record of those repeating reminders (title, list, interval and completion dates) in iCloud key-value storage in your own iCloud account, so it stays in step across your devices.
  • Settings, and the data shown by widgets, are stored on your device. Model API keys you enter are stored in the Keychain.

Deleting the app removes the data it stores on your device.

Arranging your day

On devices with Apple Intelligence, Naal arranges your days with Apple's on-device model, and nothing leaves the device for this.

Optionally, you can connect an OpenAI-compatible service or Anthropic in Settings with your own API key. Naal then sends that provider event times, recurrence, attendee counts and calendar names for the days shown. Titles, places, notes and people's names are sent only if you turn on the switch that allows it. Requests go directly from your device to the provider you chose, under your agreement with them; the developer never receives them.

Apple services

Event locations are looked up with Apple Maps to place them on a map and offer directions. Calendars, reminders and iCloud storage sync through your Apple account, and app distribution and updates are handled by the App Store and TestFlight, all under Apple's privacy policy.

Children

Naal is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new effective date.
