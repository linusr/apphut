Support

For help, bug reports or feature requests, open an issue on GitHub and start the title with "Naal". Include your OS version, your device model, and whether it has Apple Intelligence. TestFlight testers can also send feedback with a screenshot from the TestFlight app.

Getting started

  1. Open Naal and allow access to your calendars, and to Contacts for birthdays. Reminders can be turned on later in Settings → Include reminders.
  2. Days shows your days arranged; switch to the timeline with the toggle at the top.
  3. Tasks lists your reminders by day. Swipe a row to complete or move it.
  4. Add the Naal widget from the Home Screen, and allow Live Activities for a countdown to your next event.

Common questions

How do I make a reminder repeat after I complete it?
Open the task in Naal and pick an interval under Repeat after completion, or write it in the reminder's notes in any app, for example "Repeat 2 weeks after completion". When you complete it, Naal creates the next one, due that long after.

My device doesn't have Apple Intelligence.
Naal arranges your day with built-in rules. You can also connect an OpenAI-compatible service or Anthropic with your own API key in Settings.

How do I make some calendars stand out?
Choose them under Settings → Priority calendars. Their events are emphasised when Naal arranges your day.
